Top 3 Learnings

01

Make your marketing clear but uncluttered. It's much more important to advertise in the right place than to obsess over advertising volume.

02

Build the skill you want someone else to do — at least 30%. You don't need to master it, but you need enough to direct it, debug it, and own it.

03

I enjoyed the hardware process (CAD, prototyping, iterating). The next goal: 100 users on a hardware wearable at some point.
